Hitchpoint: autonomous truck-trailer

Personal project, ongoing

SimulationROS 2 HumbleGazebo HarmonicEKFLidar SLAM

A cabless tractor with a 13.6 m semi-trailer that drives a container port on its own lane map, stops for people ahead and behind, and reverses into dock doors. Every module has an evaluator against Gazebo ground truth.

  • Lidar map localisation fused into the global EKF, with a live map update for a yard that changes.
  • Benchmark of 8 open-source lidar SLAM and LIO methods (FAST-LIO2, Point-LIO, LIO-SAM, GLIM, KISS-ICP, RKO-LIO, Cartographer 3D) under GNSS loss.
  • Lidar-based hitch-angle EKF and a cascaded reversing controller with jackknife protection.

The map localisation results use an ideal map ray-cast from the world geometry; the SLAM methods run without a map. One run per scenario.

Torpedo: free-flow sewer inspection capsule

TechCore · team lead · Jan 2024 to present

Real hardwareField trialsIMUKiCadSolidWorks

A sealed capsule that drifts through sewer pipes and records its own motion. Inside the pipe there is no GNSS, so the trajectory has to come from the IMU.

  • Housing in SolidWorks: bolted, permanently sealed main body and lid, plus a screw-on cap for the power switch and USB-C.
  • Carrier PCB in KiCad: Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W, IMU, GPS, microSD logging, temperature sensor and Li-ion charging.
  • My own trajectory reconstruction from accelerometer and gyroscope signal patterns, with automatic bend detection.
  • A PC analysis tool with GPS overlay on a satellite map, 3D pipe view and synchronised video. Data comes off over Wi-Fi after a run.

The method reconstructs pipe geometry well on straights and single bends. Absolute distance along the pipe is referenced to known points; pipe-joint counting is the next step.
